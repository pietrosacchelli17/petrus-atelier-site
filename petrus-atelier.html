The name

Petrus. Atelier.

Petrus is the Latin origin of Pietro, meaning rock or stone. The name reflects a connection to architecture, materiality, craftsmanship and construction.

Atelier suggests a place of making, research and experimentation rather than a conventional architectural office. PSA stands for Pietro Sacchelli and Atelier, and the PS monogram also reads as the first and last letters of Petrus.
