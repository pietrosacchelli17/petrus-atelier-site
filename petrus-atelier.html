Petrus Atelier is interested in how design, specification and construction come together to create spaces that last.

The name

Petrus. Atelier.

Petrus is the Latin origin of Pietro, meaning rock or stone. The name reflects a connection to architecture, materiality, craftsmanship and construction.

Atelier suggests a place of making, research and experimentation rather than a conventional architectural office. PSA, the abbreviation, stands for Pietro Sacchelli and Atelier, and the PS monogram also reads as the first and last letters of Petrus.
