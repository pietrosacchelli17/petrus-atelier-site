About

Pietro Sacchelli.

Italian, based in London. Founder of Petrus Atelier, which brings together PS Gallery, an archive of architectural photography, and Keystone, software for stone and joinery.

His photographs look at modernist and contemporary architecture, historic buildings, details and natural materials, and occasionally landscape and travel: composition, geometry, light, texture and spatial relationships.
