About

Petrus Atelier.

An independent atelier by Pietro Sacchelli, working between architecture, materials and systems. The name brings together Pietro, the Etruscans and Sacchelli, and Petrus, the Latin form of Pietro: stone. Identity, heritage and material.

It is not a conventional studio but an ecosystem. Each project develops on its own while sharing one identity.

  • PS GalleryArchitectural photography and visual archive.
  • KeystoneSoftware for stone and joinery, for quotes, projects and finance.
  • Architecture and designMaterials, interiors and construction: how an idea becomes something built.

The thread is how an idea becomes something built, and how well it is seen, specified and delivered along the way.
