In short

Identity. Heritage. Stone.

Petrus brings together personal identity, Etruscan heritage and the Latin origin of Pietro, meaning stone. These references form the foundation of an independent atelier exploring architecture, materials, design and craftsmanship.

Atelier is a multidisciplinary creative practice combining architecture, materials, design and craftsmanship, with an emphasis on thoughtful, lasting work.

How it fits together

An ecosystem, not a conventional studio.

  • Petrus AtelierThe overarching creative identity: architecture, materials and design research.
  • KeystoneDigital systems and technology for the built environment.
  • PS GalleryArchitectural photography and visual archive.

Each project can develop independently while sharing a common creative identity.
